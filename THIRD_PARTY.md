## Data and models

No third-party datasets or robot models are redistributed in this repository. Synthetic clips are generated by `twinfit synth`; real clips are captured by the author and live outside the repository in `~/twinfit-data`.

Meta models (Muse Wonder, Muse Spark, SAM 3.1) are accessed through the Meta Model API under its terms; no model weights are included.
